Many agencies say "it depends" and ask you to book a call before they share a price. A low quote can still turn into a high bill if the important details are missing. Ask these 8 questions before you sign with any website developer.

## The 8 questions

1. **Is GST included in the quote?** Many quotes exclude GST, so ask for the total.
2. **Who owns the domain and hosting?** They should be registered in your name or handed over to you.
3. **Will I get the source code after the final payment?** You should own the code you paid for.
4. **How many rounds of design changes are included?** Ask for the number in writing.
5. **What happens after launch, and what does support cost?** Ask how many days of free support you get and what it costs afterwards.
6. **How does payment work?** Ask when you pay and how much, and what you get at each stage.
7. **How long will it take, and what happens if the date is missed?** Ask for a timeline and what it depends on.
8. **Who writes the content?** Ask whether text and images are included, or whether you need to send them.

## What changes the price of a website

- Number of pages
- Custom design or template
- Features like payments, bookings or logins
- Who writes the content
- How fast you need it live
