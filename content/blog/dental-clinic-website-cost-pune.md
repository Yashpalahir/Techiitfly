If you run a dental clinic in Pune and you are thinking about a website, the first question is usually "How much will it cost?" The honest answer is that it depends, and quotes can look very different from one agency to another. This guide explains what actually drives the price, so you can compare quotes properly and pay only for what your clinic needs.

## What affects the cost of a dental clinic website

| Factor | How it affects the price |
|---|---|
| Number of pages | A homepage, contact and a few treatment pages cost less than a site with 15 pages |
| Design | A clean, tested layout is faster to build than a fully custom design from scratch |
| Appointment features | A simple enquiry form is basic. A full online booking system with calendar and reminders costs more |
| Content | Writing treatment pages, doctor profiles and FAQs takes time. If you provide the text, it can cost less |
| Photos | Real photos of your clinic and team help patients trust you, but a photo shoot is an extra cost |
| Extra integrations | WhatsApp button, Google Maps and analytics are usually simple. Payment or patient-record features are larger projects |
| SEO setup | Basic SEO (page titles, descriptions, clean structure) should be included. Ongoing SEO work is separate |
| Support after launch | Some agencies include a short support period, others charge for every change |

## One-time costs and yearly costs

Many clinics are surprised by costs that come after launch. Ask about these before you sign:

- **Domain name** (your website address): usually renewed every year.
- **Hosting:** where your website lives. Some sites have free or low-cost hosting, others charge monthly.
- **Changes and updates:** new treatments, new doctor, changed timings. Ask whether small edits are included.
- **Business email:** an email address with your clinic name, if you want one.

## Do you need an expensive website?

Not always. For most small and medium clinics, patients need a few simple things: what treatments you offer, who the doctor is, where you are, when you are open, and an easy way to book. A clean website that does these well often works better than a heavy, complicated one.

If you have a bigger budget, features like a full online booking system can make sense later. Starting simple and adding features as the clinic grows is a safe approach.

## Questions to ask any agency before you hire

1. Can I get an itemised quote that shows what is included?
2. Who owns the domain and the website code? (It should be you.)
3. How long will the website take, and what do you need from me?
4. Is the website mobile-friendly and fast on a normal phone connection?
5. Is basic SEO included? What does that cover?
6. What happens after launch? Are small edits included?
7. Are there yearly or monthly charges I should know about?

## Red flags to avoid

- A quote with no breakdown.
- The agency keeps the domain or the code in its own name.
- Promises like "number 1 on Google guaranteed". Nobody can honestly guarantee that.
- Very cheap offers that use the same template and copied text for every clinic.
- Fake reviews or made-up claims added to your website. Dental and medical advertising rules in India are strict, so your content should stay factual.
