Most business owners spend weeks building a website and then never touch it again. Website maintenance means keeping your website secure, fast and up to date after launch. This checklist shows what to look after, and what a maintenance plan covers.

## What can go wrong when a website is not maintained

- A contact form stops working and nobody notices
- The pricing page still shows an old offer
- The site loads slowly on mobile and visitors leave
- Software is out of date, which leaves the site open to attacks
- The domain or SSL certificate expires without warning

None of these is a big problem if you catch it early. All of them can cost you enquiries if you do not.

## Website maintenance checklist

- **Security updates:** keep the website's software and libraries up to date.
- **Backups:** keep a recent copy so the site can be restored if something breaks.
- **Speed:** check how fast your pages load on mobile.
- **Content:** update prices, offers and contact details when they change.
- **Forms and buttons:** test that contact forms, links and WhatsApp buttons work.
- **Domain and hosting:** renew your domain, hosting and SSL on time.

## Do it yourself or use a maintenance plan?

If you are comfortable with technical updates, you can handle this checklist yourself. If you would rather spend your time on your business, a maintenance plan takes it off your plate.
