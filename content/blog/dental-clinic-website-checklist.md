When someone in Pune has a toothache or wants braces, the first thing they do is search on Google and open two or three clinic websites. If your site is slow, confusing or missing basic details, they simply move on to the next clinic. This checklist covers what a dental clinic website should have so that a visitor can find you, trust you and book an appointment without friction.

Use it to review your current site, or as a brief when you get a new one built.

## 1. A clear homepage that says who you are and where you are

Within a few seconds a visitor should know your clinic name, the area you are in (for example, Kothrud, Baner or Hinjewadi), and what you offer. Put your clinic name, locality and a main button such as "Book an appointment" at the top of the page.

## 2. A separate page for each treatment

Do not squeeze every service into one paragraph. Create one page per major treatment, such as root canal, braces, dental implants, teeth whitening and scaling. People search for specific treatments, and a dedicated page gives each one a chance to appear in Google. Each page should explain what the treatment is, who it suits, how the process works and what to expect afterwards, in simple language.

## 3. An appointment request form

A short form with name, phone number, preferred date and the problem is enough. The fewer fields, the more people will complete it. Make sure the form actually reaches you by email or WhatsApp, and test it every few weeks.

## 4. A WhatsApp button

Many patients prefer a quick WhatsApp message to a phone call or a form. A floating WhatsApp button on every page, with a pre-filled message like "Hi, I want to book an appointment", removes a lot of effort for the visitor.

## 5. Clinic timings, address and a map

Show your opening hours, holidays and full address, and embed a Google map. Keep the name, address and phone number exactly the same as on your Google Business Profile, because mismatches confuse both patients and Google.

## 6. Doctor profile and qualifications

Patients want to know who will treat them. Add each doctor's name, degrees, registration details where applicable, and areas of focus, along with a real photo. Avoid exaggerated claims such as "best dentist" or guaranteed outcomes. Dental and medical advertising in India is regulated, so keep the content factual.

## 7. Real photos of the clinic and team

Stock images feel generic. A few genuine photos of the reception, treatment room and team help nervous patients feel comfortable before they walk in.

## 8. Fast loading on mobile

Most local searches happen on phones. Compress images, avoid heavy sliders and test the site on a normal mobile connection. If a page takes more than a few seconds to open, many visitors will leave.

## 9. Mobile-friendly design

Buttons should be easy to tap, text should be readable without zooming, and the phone number should be a tap-to-call link.

## 10. Patient reviews, shown honestly

Link to or display your genuine Google reviews. Never publish fake reviews or invented testimonials. Also be careful about sharing patient names or treatment photos without written consent.

## 11. A FAQ section

Answer the questions patients always ask: how long does a root canal take, is scaling painful, what does it cost to get an appointment, do you accept insurance. FAQs help patients and also match the way people search.

## 12. Basic SEO and a connected Google Business Profile

Each page needs its own title and description, one main heading and clean URLs. Submit the site to Google Search Console and link it from your Google Business Profile. This is the foundation for appearing when someone searches "dentist near me" or "dentist in [your area]".

## FAQ

**How many pages does a dental clinic website need?**
Most clinics do well with a homepage, an about or doctor page, one page per major treatment, a contact page and a FAQ. Roughly 5 to 15 pages is a common range.

**Do I need an online booking system?**
Not necessarily. A simple appointment request form plus a WhatsApp button is enough for most small clinics, and it is easier to manage.

**Can my website help me show up on Google Maps?**
Your Google Business Profile is what appears on the map, but a website with matching name, address and phone number supports it and gives patients more information.

**How often should I update the site?**
Review timings, doctor details and the contact form every few months, and add new treatment pages or FAQs as patients ask new questions.
